How to play
  • Place exactly one pumpkin in every row, every column and every coloured patch.
  • Pumpkins can't touch each other, not even diagonally.
  • Tap a cell to cycle: empty, X, pumpkin. Drag across cells to paint X's (start on an X to erase them).
  • Clashing pumpkins glow red. Hints place a correct pumpkin but don't count toward your best time.
